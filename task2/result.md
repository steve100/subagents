2. **Chapel Hill to consider a police merger with Carrboro**
   Chapel Hill's Town Council will take up a resolution on Wednesday, Nov. 18, at 6 p.m. authorizing talks on combining the two police departments, which would bring Carrboro's 28 officers into Chapel Hill's department. The item is on the consent agenda, and Carrboro has not yet voted; its council is expected to revisit it Dec. 1 after more than 20 residents objected.
   Source: WRAL, https://www.wral.com/news/local/chapel-hill-talks-police-merger-with-carrboro-november-2026/
   Published: Nov. 18, 2026
